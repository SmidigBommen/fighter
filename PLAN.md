# Beat 'em up: plan

Decided on 2026-11-16: a scrolling beat 'em up in the style of 90s arcade brawlers, built with
TypeScript and Phaser, using free asset packs that we edit where needed. The game is original:
own characters, stages and names.

## What the genre needs

These are the rules that make a brawler feel right. They drive the architecture.

- **Three axes.** A character has `x` (along the street), `y` (depth, towards or away from the
  camera) and `z` (height while jumping). The sprite is drawn at screen position `(x, y - z)` and
  its shadow at `(x, y)`.
- **Depth tolerance.** An attack only connects when attacker and target are within about 8 px of
  each other in depth. Without this, hits land on enemies that are visibly in another lane.
- **Draw order by feet.** Everything on the walkable area is sorted by `y` each frame.
- **Frame data.** Every attack has startup, active and recovery frames, a hitbox per active frame,
  damage, knockback and hitstun. This lives in data files, not in code.
- **Hitstop.** On a hit both characters freeze for 4 to 8 frames, with a spark and a small screen
  shake. This is the largest single contributor to impact.
- **Combo chain.** Repeated attack presses step through jab, jab, punch, kick. The last hit knocks
  the enemy down. Knocked-down enemies are invulnerable until they stand up.
- **Attack tokens.** At most two enemies may attack at once. The others circle and wait. This keeps
  crowds fair.
- **Screen locks.** The camera stops at set points until the wave is cleared, then a "GO" arrow
  lets the player advance.
- **Fixed timestep.** Simulation runs at 60 ticks per second regardless of display rate, so frame
  data means the same thing on every machine.

## Technology

| Choice | Value | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Engine | Phaser 4.2.1 (`npm install phaser`) | Current release on npm. Fall back to 3.90 if a v4 problem blocks us; the official Vite template still targets 3.90. |
| Build | Vite, TypeScript, pnpm | Same setup as the hackathon project. Node 26 and pnpm 12 are installed. |
| Resolution | 426x240 internal, integer-scaled, `pixelArt: true` | The core stage art is 240 px tall. |
| Collisions | Own AABB hitbox and hurtbox checks | Phaser Arcade physics has no depth axis and no per-frame boxes. |
| Tests | Vitest on the combat simulation | Combat logic is kept free of Phaser imports so it can run headless. |
| Art editing | Aseprite (installed via Steam) | The core pack ships its `.ase` source files. |

Code layout:

```
src/sim/      pure TypeScript: entities, state machines, hit resolution, enemy AI, waves
src/data/     frame data, character definitions, stage and wave definitions
src/scenes/   Phaser scenes: Boot, Title, Stage, GameOver
src/view/     sprite, shadow, HUD and effect rendering driven by sim state
assets/raw/   downloaded packs, untouched
assets/game/  the files the game loads (cut, renamed, edited)
execution/    project tools
```

## Assets gathered

All 12 packs are CC0, downloaded to `assets/raw/` (83 MB). Licences and sources are in
`assets/CREDITS.md` and `assets/manifest.json`. Contact sheets are in `assets/preview/`.

The packs come in three art styles that do not mix on screen:

| Style | Packs | Character height | Content |
|---|---|---|---|
| A: 16-bit arcade | streets-of-fight | 47 px | Player (idle, walk, jab, punch, kick, jump, jump kick, dive kick, hurt), punk enemy (idle, walk, punch, hurt with knockdown), stage tileset, parallax layers, props (car, barrel, hydrant, signs), shadow |
| B: 8-bit console | renegade-sms, ranger-sms, soldier-nes, market-street-sms, secret-service-building-sms | 32 px | Three characters with the widest move sets (combo, headbutt, throw, carry, daze, knockdown, pick up, climb), two tilesets |
| C: small hand-drawn | business-of-rage | about 40 px | Player with a rage transformation, two street enemies, one three-layer level |

`angry-guy` (105 px, cartoon) fits none of them and is kept only as an animation reference.

Audio, usable with any style: 47 hit and punch sounds (hits-punches, punch-sfx), 5 action
chiptune tracks with title and ending themes (chiptunes-action), 12 music loops (music-loops).

**Recommendation: build on style A.** It looks closest to the arcade target and includes a full
stage. Style B is the fallback if we later want throws and weapons without drawing them.

## Gaps in style A

## Milestones

1. **Skeleton.** Vite project, Phaser boots at 426x240, stage background scrolls, fixed-timestep loop.
2. **Player movement.** Walk on the depth plane inside the walkable band, jump with shadow, y-sorting.
3. **Combat core.** Frame data format, hitboxes, hurtboxes, depth tolerance, hitstop, combo chain,
   knockdown. One training dummy. Unit tests for hit resolution.
4. **Enemies.** Punk AI: approach, take a token, attack, back off. Health, defeat, despawn.
5. **Stage flow.** Wave definitions, camera locks, "GO" prompt, breakable props, pickups.
6. **Shell.** HUD (health, score, lives), title, game over, music and sound.
7. **Content.** Enemy variants, boss, second stage, the hand-drawn gap frames.
8. **Feel pass.** Tune frame data, screen shake, sparks, enemy counts. Gamepad support, two players.

Milestones 1 to 4 give a playable prototype and need no new art.

### Status on 2026-11-17

Milestones 1 to 5 are done. Parts of 6 and 7 are in: HUD, title and end screens, music and hit
sounds, three tinted punk variants and a scaled boss.

Barrels block walking, break on one player hit and can drop food (heals 40) or points (1000).
They are placed in `src/data/stage1.ts`; pickup values are in `src/sim/objects.ts`. Pickups are
collected by walking over them.

The player's knockdown, lying and get-up frames are generated from existing frames by
`execution/build_assets.py` (`PLAYER_KNOCKDOWN`). Hand-drawn ones can replace that strip later.

Still open: second stage (7), the feel pass, gamepad and two players (8).

Run with `pnpm dev`. Controls: arrows or WASD to move, Z or J to attack, X, K or Space to jump,
attack in the air for a jump kick, down plus attack for a dive kick, H to show hitboxes.

## Tools

| Command | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `execution/fetch_assets.py [ids]` | Download packs listed in `assets/sources.toml` from OpenGameArt, unpack them, regenerate the manifest and credits. Add a pack by adding an entry to the TOML file. |
| `execution/contact_sheet.py -o out.png "glob" ...` | Put matching PNG files on one labelled image for visual comparison. |
| `execution/build_assets.py [sprites stage fx audio]` | Build `assets/game/` from `assets/raw/`: re-anchored sprite strips, the composed stage 1 image, the generated font and spark, converted audio. The stage layout is the `STAGE1_*` constants in the script. Also `pnpm assets`. |
| `node execution/smoke.mjs [--seconds 30] [--out dir]` | With `pnpm dev` running, a bot plays the game in headless Chromium, saves screenshots and fails on page errors. |
| `pnpm test` | Vitest on the combat simulation in `src/sim/`. |
